# Maglev train, electromagnetic suspension (main 2)

A tabletop EMS train: electromagnets under the rail pull the vehicle up, a controller holds the gap.

- cfsem: coil fields and forces against the rail; inductance for the coil dynamics.
- CortenForge: vehicle and track simulation, gap controller, printable track and vehicle parts.
- Showpiece: physics plus control, with a printable track.
